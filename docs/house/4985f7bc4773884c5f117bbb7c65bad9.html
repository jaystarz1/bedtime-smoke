The Five Stepping Stones

In Hush Hollow, when the light goes gold and low, the stream sings a small song to itself, over and over, all evening long.

Pup stood at the edge of the water. Five flat stones lay across the stream like five little steps. Pup had never gone across them. Not once. Not yet.

"I want to," said Pup, in a small voice. "But the water is going by so fast."

Fox came and stood beside Pup, side by side, near the edge of the singing water.

"Then we will go the slow way," said Fox. "One small step, soft and slow."

On the last stone sat Frog, round and green and comfortable, with a throat going in and out like a tiny bellows.

"Brumm," said Frog. "I will be right here the whole time."

So Pup put one foot on the first stone. The stone was warm from the long day of sun.

"One small step, soft and slow," said Fox.

Then the second stone. Pup held Fox's hand, and the water went by underneath, silver and busy, singing its song.

Then the third stone, right in the middle, with the whole Hollow around them and the evening coming down soft as a blanket.

Pup stopped there a moment. Fox waited. Nobody hurried. Nobody ever hurries in the Hollow at night.

"One small step, soft and slow," said Pup this time, without any help at all. Then the fourth stone. Then the fifth.

And then the other side.

"Brumm," said Frog, pleased. "You did the whole brave thing, and you did it slowly, which is the best way of all."

Pup did not cheer. Pup stood in the cool grass with a warm, quiet, proud feeling all the way through, like a small lamp lit somewhere inside.

Then home. Back over the five stones, easy now, the way a thing turns easy once you have done it once. Back up the path. Back to the blankets, pulled up warm.

Wherever you go, and however far you wander, you go the same way. One small step, soft and slow. And the Hollow keeps a warm place for you the whole time.

The stream sang on, softer now. Fox yawned. Pup yawned. Frog settled into the mud like a small green pillow.

Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Frog. Goodnight, moon. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
