Warm and Near

In Hush Hollow, at the end of the day, the Garden went quiet and grey-blue. Bunny and Hedgehog sat on the step with one soft blanket over their knees, side by side.

"Warm and near, warm and near," said Bunny.

"Warm and near," said Hedgehog.

Then, over by the watering can, a small sound. A little mew.

It was Honey, the cat of the Garden. Her fur was damp from the grass. Her tail hung low, her whiskers were beaded with dew, and she had no warm spot at all.

That was a job too big for one.

So they did it together, slow. Bunny lifted one corner of the blanket. Hedgehog lifted the other. They carried it down the step, a little and a little, together and slow, and laid it flat on the moss beside the beans.

The blanket was long and the step was tall, and one on their own would have needed help. But two can carry a blanket. Two go slow, and slow gets there.

Honey stepped on. She turned around once. She turned around twice. Then she settled in the middle and began to purr, low as a kettle.

Bunny sat on one side. Hedgehog sat on the other. They folded the blanket up over Honey's back, and up over their own knees, and there was enough. There was even a little extra. Hedgehog smoothed one corner down. Bunny tucked the other under, gentle as gentle.

"Warm and near, warm and near," said Bunny.

"Warm and near," said Hedgehog.

And here is the funny thing. The blanket was warmer with three under it than it had been with two. Warmth is like that. Give a bit away, and more comes back.

The beans nodded. A cricket sang one slow song, and then stopped. Honey's purring went softer and softer, the way a purr goes soft when a cat is nearly asleep.

Their eyes went heavy. Their hands went still. The little job was done, and done well, and nothing in the Garden needed doing anymore.

"Warm and near," said Bunny, very quietly.

"Warm and near," said Hedgehog.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Honey, purring under the blanket. Goodnight, moon. Goodnight, Garden, with your sleeping beans and your quiet step.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

Sleep now. Warm and near.
