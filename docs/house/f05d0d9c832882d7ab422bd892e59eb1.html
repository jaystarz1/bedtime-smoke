The Quiet Pile in the Wood

Evening came soft to Hush Hollow. The Wood went slow and golden and then went blue.

Fox and Mouse sat on the step outside the Hollow, side by side, with one big quilt over four knees.

"The dark is coming," said Mouse, in a very small voice.

Fox leaned in. "You are safe," said Fox. "You are safe, you are safe."

Mrs. Squirrel came down the old trunk with her tail curled high behind her, and she carried a cup of warm milk in both paws. She tucked it into Mouse's hands.

"Sip slow," said Mrs. Squirrel. "Slow, slow, slow."

So Mouse sipped slow. And the warm went all the way down.

"Now everybody goes to bed," whispered Fox.

And the beetle went to bed under a leaf.

"And the beetle is asleep, all snug in the Wood," said Fox.

The little brown birds tucked their heads.

"And the birds are asleep, and the beetle is asleep, all snug in the Wood."

Mouse smiled.

"You are safe," said Fox. "You are safe, you are safe."

The crickets slowed and stopped.

"And the crickets are asleep, and the birds are asleep, and the beetle is asleep, all snug in the Wood."

Mrs. Squirrel yawned an enormous squirrel yawn and laid her tail over her nose like a blanket.

"And Mrs. Squirrel is asleep," said Mouse, "and the crickets are asleep, and the birds are asleep, and the beetle is asleep, all snug in the Wood."

The wind lay down in the tall grass.

"And the wind is asleep, and Mrs. Squirrel is asleep, and the crickets are asleep, and the birds are asleep, and the beetle is asleep, all snug in the Wood."

Mouse leaned on Fox's shoulder. The quilt was warm. The cup was empty. The dark was only the Wood with its eyes shut.

"You are safe," said Fox. "You are safe, you are safe."

"I know," said Mouse. And Mouse did know.

They went in through the round door of the Hollow, soft and slow. They put the cup on the shelf. They pulled the quilt up to their chins.

Goodnight, beetle. Goodnight, birds. Goodnight, crickets. Goodnight, wind in the tall grass.

Goodnight, Mrs. Squirrel, with your tail over your nose.

Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Fox.

Goodnight, moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
