Goodnight, Hush Hollow

The Meadow went gold, and then soft, the way it always does.

Pup and Fox sat down in the tall grass where the clover grows thick. The first fireflies came up out of the green, one, then two, then a slow drifting many.

Then Honey found them. She padded through the clover with her tail high, and curled once around Fox's ankle, and once around Pup's, purring already, the way she does.

Both of them wanted the same warm thing.

"Honey can sit in my lap," said Pup.

"Honey can sit in mine," said Fox.

Honey sat down in the grass between them, round and patient, and waited.

"Then we will take turns," said Fox. "Your turn, my turn, soft and slow."

So Pup went first. Honey stepped into Pup's lap, turned around twice, kneaded a little, and settled. Her purr went right through Pup's middle like a small warm engine. Fox counted fireflies while the turn went by, and the waiting was not so long, because a firefly is a good thing to count.

Your turn, my turn, soft and slow.

Then it was Fox's turn. Honey stretched long and slow, stepped over, and curled into Fox with her chin on Fox's arm. She gave Fox the slow blink, the one that means I love you. Pup lay back in the clover and counted stars, and the waiting was not so long that way either.

Your turn, my turn, soft and slow.

"It lasts longer this way," said Pup.

"It does," said Fox.

So they took turns until the fireflies were low and the grass was cool and the whole Meadow had gone quiet. Honey purred for both of them, one lap and then the other. Nobody hurried. Nobody was left out.

Then they went along the Lane together, the short soft way toward bed, with Honey walking between them, where a cat can be near to everyone at once.

Pup yawned. Fox yawned too.

They lay down under the blanket, side by side, warm all the way through, with Honey settling at their feet and purring smaller and smaller and smaller.

There is enough, my love. There is always enough. Your turn comes, and it comes soft, and it comes slow.

Your turn. My turn. Soft and slow.

Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Honey, purring at our feet. Goodnight, clover, goodnight, fireflies. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
