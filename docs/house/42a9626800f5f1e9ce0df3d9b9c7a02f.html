Home, Home, Soft and Slow

In Hush Hollow, the day went soft at the edges.

Down at the stream, Mouse and Bunny had spent the whole long afternoon. They had stacked flat stones into a little tower. They had floated three leaf boats and named all three of them Bill. They had dipped their hands in the cold bright water until their fingers went pink.

Now the light turned gold, and their legs went slow.

"Time to go home," said Mouse.

"Home," said Bunny, with a yawn as wide as a teacup.

Home, home, soft and slow.

Frog came up onto his flat green stone. His throat puffed. His long legs folded neat beneath him.

"Goodnight, you two," said Frog. "I will settle right here."

So the reeds leaned over and rested. The stream went singing on, quiet and quieter, hush.

And the reeds are asleep, and the stones are asleep, all snug in the stream.

Mouse and Bunny went up the path. Not fast. Just steady, the way tired people go.

Home, home, soft and slow.

They went past the blackberry tangle, where a small brown bird tucked her head beneath her wing. They went past the moss bank, where the beetles folded up like tiny brown books.

And the bird is asleep, and the beetles are asleep, and the reeds are asleep, and the stones are asleep, all snug in Hush Hollow.

Then up the last soft hill.

And there it was. One warm window, glowing gold through the trees. Someone had left the lamp on for them, the way someone always did.

Home, home, soft and slow.

The door opened. Two pairs of arms came around them, big and warm and smelling of bread. The arms held on a little longer than they needed to.

There was bread. There was a blanket. There was a low, kind voice saying, "There you are. There you are."

The Moon came up over Hush Hollow and rested her light on the windowsill.

Mouse yawned. Bunny yawned. The lamp went low.

And Mouse is asleep, and Bunny is asleep, and Frog is asleep, and the bird is asleep, and the beetles are asleep, and the reeds and the stones and the stream are asleep, all snug in Hush Hollow.

Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Frog, on your flat green stone. Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, warm window, and goodnight, warm arms.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
