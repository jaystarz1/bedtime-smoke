Gentle, Gentle

In Hush Hollow, the meadow went quiet and blue. The grass leaned over and grew still. One firefly lit, then two, then a soft handful more.

Hedgehog and Pup sat in the warm grass beside Honey, who was big and slow and covered in soft fur. Honey hummed a low hum. It went out over the meadow like a blanket.

Pup held a little reed basket. The handle had come undone. Three reeds hung loose in the grass. Pup's shoulders went down.

"It broke," said Pup.

"Then we will mend it," said Honey, in her low warm rumble. "Gentle, gentle, all is well."

Hedgehog sat down beside Pup and took up one loose reed. Over, and under. Over, and under. Slow as the hum.

"Like this?" said Pup, and took up a reed too.

"Just like that," said Honey. "Gentle, gentle, all is well."

The first reed went in. The basket held it. The second reed went in, and the handle began to curve again. The third reed was the slowest. Pup's hands went carefully, and Hedgehog held the basket still, and Honey kept her low hum going over them both.

And then the handle was a handle again.

Pup lifted the basket by its mended handle. It held. It held the whole way up.

"It's better than before," said Pup.

"It is mended," said Honey, "and mending takes time, and we had time."

So they filled the basket with clover, slow handful by slow handful, and carried it home through the grass. The fireflies came along a little way, and then stayed in the meadow to shine.

Back in Hush Hollow, the basket sat by the door with the clover in it. Hedgehog yawned. Pup yawned right after. Honey settled down, warm and wide, and they leaned on her, one on each side.

"Will you stay?" said Pup, small and sleepy.

"I'm here," said Honey. "Soft and safe. Gentle, gentle, all is well."

Honey lifted her paw toward the sky. "There's the Moon," she said. "She keeps the night company. She'll be there tomorrow too."

Outside, the grass went still. Inside, the breathing went slow. Hedgehog's eyes closed. Pup's eyes closed.

Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Honey, humming low. Goodnight, little mended basket by the door. Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

Sleep now. Gentle, gentle, all is well.
