The Last Blackberry

In Hush Hollow, down at the Pond, the evening came in soft and slow.

Fox and Mouse sat side by side on the wide flat stone, with a little leaf cup between them. Inside the leaf cup was one last blackberry. Just one. The darkest, sweetest, best one of the whole day.

Fox slid the leaf cup over to Mouse.

"For you," said Fox. "For you, for you."

"But it's the best one," said Mouse.

"That's why," said Fox.

Mouse held the leaf cup in two hands, and the berry shone like a tiny dark star.

Then the water went ring, ring, ring, and up came Mr. Trout, silver from nose to tail, with his fins waving slow in the cool green shadows.

"Good evening, small ones," rumbled Mr. Trout. "That is a fine berry."

Mouse held the leaf cup out over the water.

"For you," said Mouse. "For you, for you."

Mr. Trout took the smallest bite a big fish ever took. Then he tipped the berry gently back into the cup.

"Now it belongs to everybody," he said. "That's the sweetest way."

So Fox and Mouse shared what was left, one bite and one bite, and the sweetness stayed on and on, warmer than the berry, warm as a blanket, warm all the way home.

Up the soft path they went, past the reeds, past the ferns, into the Hollow, where the lamps were low and the beds were waiting.

And the berry is finished, all snug in the Hollow.

And the leaf cup is folded, and the berry is finished, all snug in the Hollow.

And the reeds are dreaming, and the leaf cup is folded, and the berry is finished, all snug in the Hollow.

And Mr. Trout is drifting, and the reeds are dreaming, and the leaf cup is folded, and the berry is finished, all snug in the Hollow.

And Mouse is yawning, and Fox is yawning, and Mr. Trout is drifting, and the reeds are dreaming, and the leaf cup is folded, and the berry is finished, all snug in the Hollow.

And the reeds are quiet now. And the water is quiet now. And Fox and Mouse are quiet now, all snug in the Hollow.

The Moon is up over the water, quiet and kind.

Hush now. Hush, hush.

Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Mr. Trout.

Goodnight, Moon over the water.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
