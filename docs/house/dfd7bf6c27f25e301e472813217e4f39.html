Side by Side Again

In Hush Hollow, at the soft green edge of the meadow, Bunny and Hedgehog kept a little basket between them. Every evening they filled it with clover heads for Honey, who hummed among the flowers all day long.

But this evening the basket sat empty, and the two friends sat apart.

It began with a small mix-up. Bunny thought Hedgehog had gone for the clover. Hedgehog thought Bunny had. So nobody went, and the basket stayed empty, and each of them sat on their own side of the meadow with a small ache under the heart.

Honey came by, low and slow, her wings a warm little sound. She settled on the rim of the basket. She did not scold. Bees never scold at bedtime.

"It's a big basket for one," said Honey. "It's a small basket for two."

Bunny stood up. Hedgehog stood up. Through the tall grass they went, one step, then another step, until the grass between them was gone.

"I'm sorry," said Bunny, soft.

"I'm sorry," said Hedgehog, softer.

And they were side by side again.

Then the little job began. Together, together, soft and slow. Bunny bent a clover stem. Hedgehog held the basket steady. One clover head in. Then another. A little more, and a little more, together and slow.

The meadow went dim and blue around them. Crickets tuned up in the grass. The two friends worked on, side by side again, and the basket grew fuller and heavier and sweeter, and neither one hurried at all.

"Done," said Bunny at last.

"Done," said Hedgehog.

They carried it between them, one handle each, over to Honey's old log, and set it down so slowly the clover never rustled. Honey hummed her thank-you hum, and the whole log hummed back.

Then home through Hush Hollow, tired and glad, the way you feel when a thing is set right.

They sat down on the moss by the door. Their hands were sticky with clover. Their eyes were heavy. Above them the Moon came up over the meadow, quiet and kind, keeping her watch on the grass, the log, the basket, the two friends.

Side by side again, and slower now, and slower.

A yawn from Bunny. A yawn from Hedgehog. The clover smell drifting soft over everything.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Honey, humming low in her log.

Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
