Down at the Stream

Down in Hush Hollow the day had gone quiet, and the water went by soft and slow.

Bunny and Hedgehog carried their blanket down to the Stream, the way they did when the sky turned to evening.

Frog sat on his flat wet stone. His green throat puffed. His webbed toes were spread wide. But his song had stopped.

"I have lost my little smooth stone," said Frog. "The blue one. I keep it right here, and now here is empty."

Bunny sat down beside him. Hedgehog sat down too.

"Then we will find it," said Bunny. "Together, together, soft and slow."

So they began.

Look low, look slow.

They went along the edge of the water where the reeds lean over. Hedgehog moved one reed, and then another, and then another. Bunny turned the round pebbles over, one at a time, and set each one back where it had been.

The Moon came up over the Hollow, and the stars came out above the water, one and one and one. The crickets kept a small steady beat. The Stream went by, soft and slow.

Look low, look slow.

Frog hopped a little way and lifted the edge of his lily pad. Nothing there. Bunny reached into the cool wet sand, took up a handful, and let it run out again. Nothing there. Hedgehog knelt where the water was shallow and moved the small stones aside, one, and one, and one.

Together, together, soft and slow.

A little more, and a little more, together and slow.

And then Hedgehog's hand touched something round. Something smooth. Something blue.

"Here," said Hedgehog, quiet as anything. "Here it is."

Frog took his little blue stone in his webbed toes and held it against his soft green throat. "Oh," he said. "Oh, thank you. Thank you both."

He sang one low happy note, the way he does at the end of a good day. And the Stream went by, soft and slow.

Look low, look slow. And there it was.

Then they folded the blanket and walked back up the path into the Hollow, tired and glad, the way you are when a little job is done.

Bunny yawned. Hedgehog yawned too.

Down they lay, side by side, warm under the blanket, with the Moon above the Hollow and the water still going by below.

Goodnight, Frog, with your blue stone safe. Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, Stream. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

Sleep now, soft and slow.
