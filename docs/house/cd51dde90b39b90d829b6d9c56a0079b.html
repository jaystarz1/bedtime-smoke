One Star, Two Stars

Down in Hush Hollow the willow leaned low, and under the willow the stream ran slow. The water went over the stones without sound. The evening came soft to the cool of the ground.

But Fox and Mouse had the day still awake in their hands, a whole busy day full of games and of plans. Their eyes would not settle. Their toes would not rest. The day kept on humming inside of each chest.

So down to the water the two of them came, and Frog on his lily pad called them by name. He puffed his green throat like a slow little drum. "Sit here," said the Frog. "Let the quiet things come."

One star, two stars, soft and slow. That is the way that the long days go.

"Count one for the stream and the bends that it takes. Count two for the reed and the ring that it makes. Count three for the pebble, all smooth in your hand. Count four for the cool of the wet river sand."

So Mouse counted slowly. And Fox counted too. The stream took the numbers and carried them through. And every soft number they gave to the stream went out with the water and off like a dream.

One star, two stars, soft and slow. That is the way that the long days go.

Frog blinked his gold eyes. His webbed toes went still. "Now breathe with the water. Breathe out. Breathe until the day that was busy has drifted downstream, and all that is left of it turns into dream."

So Fox breathed. And Mouse breathed. And out went the day, around the near bend and then softly away. Their eyes had grown heavy, their breathing gone deep, and the stream said the last of the evening to sleep.

Then up the small path where the lamp glow was gold, back home to the Hollow, the blankets, the fold. One star at the window, and then there were two. The dark came down gentle. The night came down blue. Two beds in the dark and the hush of a door. The day has been counted. It asks you no more.

One star, two stars, soft and slow. That is the way that the long days go.

Goodnight to the water. Goodnight to the stones. Goodnight to the Frog and his low sleepy tones. Goodnight to the Moon and her quiet, kind light. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Mouse. Sleep well and sleep tight. Goodnight, Hush Hollow. The lamps are turned low. One star, two stars. Now sleep, soft and slow.
