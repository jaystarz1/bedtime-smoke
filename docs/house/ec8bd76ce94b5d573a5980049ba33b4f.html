Out and Home

The Wood went quiet. In Hush Hollow the windows glowed one by one, small and yellow, and Bear sat on his step with his heavy paws folded.

Hedgehog and Pup sat beside him, snug under one blanket.

"Bear," said Hedgehog, "what is out past the last lamp?"

"More Wood," said Bear. "Ferns. A grey stone. A puddle with the sky in it."

"Could we go and see?" said Pup.

"We could," said Bear. "Out and home, soft and slow."

So they went, the three of them, no faster than that.

First came the ferns. They stood tall and still, and the wind moved through them like breathing.

"They're only ferns," said Bear. "They stand out here all night. They don't mind the dark."

"I mind it a little," said Hedgehog.

"I know," said Bear. "I'm here."

Next came the grey stone, warm on top from the long day. Pup put both hands flat on it and held them there.

"The dark didn't take the warm away," said Pup.

"No," said Bear. "The dark keeps things. It doesn't take them."

Then came the puddle, round and quiet at the edge of the path. In it lay the Moon, holding her light steady, and a scatter of little stars, and three faces above it.

"That's us," said Hedgehog.

"That's us," said Bear. "And that's as far as we go. Out and home, soft and slow."

They turned around. The way back was the way they came, only softer, because they knew it now. Past the stone. Past the ferns. Past the last lamp, and there was Hush Hollow with its windows still glowing, waiting exactly where they left it.

Bear opened the door. The blanket was still warm on the step, so they brought it in.

"We went out," said Pup, slow and heavy now.

"You did," said Bear. "And you came home. Out and home, soft and slow."

Hedgehog leaned on Bear's shoulder. Pup's eyes went to a squint, then to nothing at all. Bear's low voice went lower, the way a fire goes down and keeps its warmth.

Goodnight, ferns. Goodnight, grey warm stone. Goodnight, puddle with the sky in it. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Bear.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow. Sleep now, soft and slow.
