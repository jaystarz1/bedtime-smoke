Out and Home

In Hush Hollow the kettle goes quiet and low, and the meadow turns gold in the last of the glow. Fox and Mouse sit on the step where the clover grows tall, and the day folds its edges, and that is all.

But the meadow goes on, and the meadow goes far, past the clover, the gate, and the first little star. "Just a little way out," says Mouse. "Then a little way back." "Out and home," says Fox. "Down the soft grassy track."

Then Honey comes padding, warm-footed and slow, with a purr in her chest like a kettle set low. She blinks the slow blink that means I will come too, and they step off the step, through the clover and dew.

Out past the fence post. Out past the stone. Never far. Never fast. And never alone. The crickets tune up in the grass where they roam. Out and home, soft and slow. Out and home.

A firefly lifts. Then another. Then ten, and they dim and they drift and they brighten again. Mouse holds up a hand, and one rests there a while, then it lifts itself off, and Mouse keeps the smile.

Then Fox turns and points at the Hollow's warm light, one small golden square in the blue of the night. "That's just far enough," says Fox. "Now we go." And they turn themselves round. Out and home, soft and slow.

Back past the fence post. Back past the stone. Never far. Never fast. And never alone. Honey's tail lifts up straight as a stem of tall grass, and the Moon is above them. The soft minutes pass.

And the door of the Hollow is warm and is wide, and the little way out tucks itself up inside. There is milk. There's a blanket. There's Honey's soft purr, and the meadow goes quiet again where they were.

Out and home, soft and slow. Out and home.

Fox yawns a slow yawn. Then Mouse yawns one too, and Honey curls small where the blanket is blue. Her purr goes so quiet it thins into air, and the fireflies dim in the meadow out there.

Goodnight, little Honey, curled up in the fold. Goodnight to the meadow, gone quiet and gold. Goodnight, little Fox. Goodnight, little Mouse. Goodnight to the Moon on the roof of the house. Goodnight, Hush Hollow. The long day is through. Out and home, soft and slow. Goodnight to you.
