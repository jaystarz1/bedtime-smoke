Room at the Pond

The Hollow went quiet the way it always does, one small light at a time.

Down at the pond the water lay flat and warm and still. Hedgehog sat on the mossy bank with a blanket over both knees. Frog sat beside Hedgehog on his wide green stone, throat going in and out, slow and easy.

"Someone is standing back there," said Hedgehog.

Frog turned his big gold eyes toward the reeds. "That is Pup," said Frog. "New to the Hollow. New to the dark."

Pup stood at the edge of the tall grass, holding one sleeve tight, not coming any nearer.

"Come along, come along," said Hedgehog. "There is room."

Pup did not move. "It is dark by the water," said Pup, very small.

"It is," said Frog, in his low pond voice. "And I am here. And Hedgehog is here. Soft and safe, all night long."

Hedgehog moved over and smoothed a spot in the moss with one hand. A soft spot. A warm spot. Just the right size for somebody new.

"Come along, come along," said Hedgehog. "I kept you the softest bit."

Pup came one slow step, and then another, and sat down in the place made ready.

"Well now," said Frog. "That is better."

The fireflies came up out of the reeds, a few and then a few more, little green lamps drifting over the water. One settled on Pup's blanket and stayed there.

"They do that every night," said Frog.

"Every night?" said Pup.

"Every single night," said Frog. "And I am here for all of them."

So the three of them sat side by side, warm under one long blanket, while the pond made its small sounds. Nothing hurried anybody. The dark was not a big thing anymore. It was a quiet room with everyone in it.

After a while Frog walked them to the path.

"Come along, come along," said Hedgehog, and Pup came along, right beside, all the way up the hill to the Hollow, where the beds were turned down and waiting.

Pup yawned. Hedgehog yawned too. Blankets up to chins.

"Am I one of the Hollow now?" said Pup.

"You were, the moment you sat down," said Hedgehog. "Soft and safe. Sleep now."

Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Pup, with a firefly still on the blanket. Goodnight, Frog, on his wide green stone. Goodnight, pond. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
