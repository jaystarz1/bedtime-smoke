The Stream That Kept Giving

In Hush Hollow, the night came down soft and slow. Down by the Stream, the water went hush, hush, hush over the smooth grey stones.

Hedgehog sat on the bank with a basket. Inside the basket were seven small blueberries, round and blue and sweet.

Pup came and sat beside them.

"Are those all you have?" said Pup.

"All I have," said Hedgehog. "If I share them, I will have hardly any."

"I'm here," said Pup, soft and safe. "Let's see."

So Hedgehog gave Pup one blueberry. One.

And Frog came hopping up the wet stones, with his round green eyes and his throaty little song. Frog carried a lily pad, wide as a plate.

"For your basket," croaked Frog. "It keeps things cool."

Now there were berries, and a lily pad too.

More and more, soft and slow.

Hedgehog gave Frog a blueberry. Frog sang thank you, low and glad, and hopped off into the reeds.

Then the reeds rustled. Frog came back with three brown nuts held tight, and set them down.

"From the far bank," said Frog. "Nobody wanted them but me, and I have plenty."

More and more, soft and slow.

Hedgehog looked into the basket. Berries. A lily pad. Three brown nuts. It was fuller than before, not emptier.

"Take another," said Hedgehog, and gave one to Pup.

Pup put a smooth grey stone in the basket, the shiniest one on the whole bank.

"I'm here," said Pup, soft and safe. "And the basket is heavy now."

It was. They carried it together, one handle each, back along the water. The Stream went hush, hush, hush beside them all the way home.

At the door of the little house, they set the basket down and shared everything out. There was enough. There was more than enough.

More and more, soft and slow.

Hedgehog yawned. Pup yawned too, a big one, the kind that comes at the end.

"I don't want the day to be over," said Hedgehog.

"Then I'll sit right here," said Pup, "soft and safe, until you sleep."

Outside, the Moon came up over Hush Hollow, and the stars stood quiet around her.

Goodnight, Frog, down in the reeds. Goodnight, Stream, going hush, hush, hush. Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
