Goodnight, Meadow

In Hush Hollow the sky went soft and blue-gray, and the meadow grass leaned over to rest.

Mouse came down the path with a blanket folded over one arm. Bunny came behind, hurrying, with too many thoughts.

"My thinking is going fast," said Bunny. "It goes and goes and it will not sit down."

"Then we will sit down with it," said Mouse.

So they sat in the warm grass, side by side, where Honey lay curled with her nose tucked under her paw. Honey hummed her low sleepy hum.

"Breathe with me," said Mouse. "In, and out. Soft and slow."

Bunny breathed in. Bunny breathed out.

"In and out. Soft and slow."

The crickets stopped their little song, one and then another.

"And the crickets are quiet," said Mouse, "all snug in the meadow."

They breathed again. The clover folded its petals for the night, the way clover does.

"And the clover is quiet, and the crickets are quiet, all snug in the meadow."

Bunny's shoulders came down a bit. The fast thoughts walked slower now.

Honey's hum went softer, and softer, and stopped. Her breathing went long and warm.

"And Honey is quiet, and the clover is quiet, and the crickets are quiet, all snug in the meadow."

"In and out. Soft and slow," said Bunny, and this time Bunny said it first.

The wind came through the tall grass one gentle time, and lay down too.

"And the wind is quiet, and Honey is quiet, and the clover is quiet, and the crickets are quiet, all snug in the meadow."

The path went dark and soft. Up in the willow, the small brown birds tucked their beaks down and stopped their chattering.

"And the birds are quiet, and the wind is quiet, and Honey is quiet, and the clover is quiet, and the crickets are quiet, all snug in the meadow."

Bunny yawned a very wide yawn. Mouse yawned right after, because yawns are like that.

"Where did the worry go?" said Bunny, slow and blurry.

"It floated off," said Mouse. "It was only visiting."

They carried the blanket back up the path, home to the Hollow, where the beds were waiting. Bunny lay down. Mouse lay down. Honey came in behind them and curled by the door with a rumbly sigh.

The Moon was out over Hush Hollow, quiet and kind, keeping the light.

In and out. Soft and slow.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Honey. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
