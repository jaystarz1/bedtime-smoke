Gentle, Gentle, All Is Well

The lamps of the Hollow burned honey and low, and the wind in the treetops went soft, and went slow. Fox and Mouse buttoned their coats to the chin and went down the green path where the night settles in. Past the ferns, past the moss, where the small creatures dwell. Gentle, gentle, all is well.

On the flat mossy stone where the lily pads float sat Frog, and beside him, his little reed boat. The grass that had tied it had come all undone, and the reeds lay apart on the bank, one by one. Frog said not a word. Frog's green throat did not drum. He sat by the pieces, and his hum would not come. Mouse knelt in the rushes and said, "Frog, we can mend." Fox said, "We have time, and the time is our friend." And the wind in the reeds rang as soft as a bell. Gentle, gentle, all is well.

They gathered the reeds and they laid them in rows, one and then one, the way careful work goes. Mouse held them steady. Fox wrapped the grass tight, over and under, and slow as the night. They did not go quickly. They did not go far. They worked the way patient and careful things are. One knot came out crooked. They did not mind. They loosened it, tied it, unhurried and kind. Mouse smoothed every reed till it lay where it should. Fox pressed the last knot, and the last knot was good. A snail went to sleep in the curl of its shell. Gentle, gentle, all is well.

They set the boat down where the still water lay. It floated. It rocked. And it wanted to stay. The mend hardly showed. It held snug. It held tight, and the boat sat as steady and true as the night. Frog climbed aboard with a low, happy hum, and his throat went again like a small, warm drum. The rushes leaned in where the soft dark fell. Gentle, gentle, all is well.

Then Fox and Mouse walked back up the hill, past the moss, past the ferns, and the world was still. The lamps of the Hollow burned honey and low, and their blankets lay turned back, soft as snow.

Goodnight, little boat on the quiet dark pond. Goodnight, Frog, on your flat mossy stone. Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Moon, up over the trees. Goodnight, Hush Hollow. Sleep soft now. Sleep slow. Gentle, gentle, all is well.
