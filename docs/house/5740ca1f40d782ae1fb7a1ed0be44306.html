Goodnight, Hush Hollow

The sun has gone down over the meadow. The grass is warm and bent and soft.

Mouse and Bunny sit side by side in the tall grass. Their blanket is spread out flat. Their two cups are empty. Almost, almost time for bed.

But first, says Mouse, we say goodnight to everything.

Yes, says Bunny. To everything.

So they begin.

Goodnight, little clover. Goodnight, small stone by the path.

Goodnight, goodnight, soft and slow.

They turn to the pond. The water is still and dark and round. One ripple goes out, and then no more.

Goodnight, quiet water. Goodnight, reeds along the edge.

Goodnight, goodnight, soft and slow.

They turn to the hill. The hill is a long low shape against the sky. Somewhere up there the crickets keep their tiny song going.

Goodnight, big hill. Goodnight, crickets in the grass.

Goodnight, goodnight, soft and slow.

Robin comes down and settles on the fence post beside them. She folds her wings. She tucks her small brown head down until she is round as a berry.

Goodnight, Robin, says Mouse.

Robin gives one soft sleepy note. That is her goodnight back.

Now the meadow is dim. The path home is pale and easy to follow. Mouse and Bunny fold the blanket. They carry it between them, one corner each, all the way to the door.

Inside, the lamp is low and warm.

Goodnight, little lamp. Goodnight, two cups on the shelf. Goodnight, blanket, folded and put away.

Goodnight, goodnight, soft and slow.

Mouse lies down. Bunny lies down. The pillows are cool. The quilt is heavy in the good way.

The Moon is up over the meadow tonight. {{moon}}

Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, stars keeping watch. Goodnight, grass. Goodnight, pond. Goodnight, hill.

Goodnight, Robin on your fence post.

Goodnight, Bunny.

Goodnight, Mouse.

Goodnight, goodnight, soft and slow.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

The whole world is tucked in now. Every small thing has been named and wished well. Now there is only the quiet, and the warm dark, and sleep.
