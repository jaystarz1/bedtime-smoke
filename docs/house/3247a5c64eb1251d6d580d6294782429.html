Goodnight, Cove

Evening came soft to Hush Hollow. Fox and Mouse went down the little path to the Cove, where the water lay flat and warm.

"Just you and me," said Fox, "soft and slow."

"Just you and me," said Mouse, "soft and slow."

They wanted nothing grand. They only wanted the evening together. So they sat side by side on the smooth stone and let the quiet come.

One small light blinked on above the water. Then another. Then another, until the sky was speckled and kind.

Wink the firefly drifted down on tiny glowing wings, blinking his slow gold blink, and settled on the warm stone beside them.

"Room for one more?" he blinked.

"Room for one more," said Fox and Mouse. "Just you and me and Wink, soft and slow."

The water hummed a low hum. Fox hummed it too. Mouse hummed it too. Wink blinked in time.

One by one, the reeds leaned over and stopped their whispering.

And the reeds are asleep, all snug in the Cove.

A sleepy duck tucked her bill beneath her wing. She wiggled her feathers down into a soft brown pile and closed her eyes.

And the duck is asleep, and the reeds are asleep, all snug in the Cove.

The little boat by the shore rocked once, and then no more.

And the boat is asleep, and the duck is asleep, and the reeds are asleep, all snug in the Cove.

A cricket slowed his chirp. Chirp. Chirp. Chiiiiirp. And then nothing, only breathing.

And the cricket is asleep, and the boat is asleep, and the duck is asleep, and the reeds are asleep, all snug in the Cove.

Wink's gold blink grew slow, and slower, until it was only a warm little glow, like a nightlight left on for a friend.

And Wink is asleep, and the cricket is asleep, and the boat is asleep, and the duck is asleep, and the reeds are asleep, all snug in the Cove.

Then Fox and Mouse went home along the little path, back up to the Hollow, hand in hand.

"That was a good evening," said Mouse.

"That was a good evening," said Fox. "Just you and me, soft and slow."

They settled into their warm beds. A quilt over Mouse. A quilt over Fox. The Moon rested her gentle light on the windowsill, and the stars kept the watch.

One yawn. Then another yawn. Then a very long, very slow yawn that ended in nothing at all.

Goodnight, Wink. Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Fox.

Goodnight, Moon, and goodnight, stars.

Goodnight, Cove, all quiet and warm.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
