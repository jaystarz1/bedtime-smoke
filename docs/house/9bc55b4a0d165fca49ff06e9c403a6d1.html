Goodnight, Pond

Night comes to Hush Hollow soft and slow.

Here is the path. Here is the gate of reeds. Here is the Pond, dark and round and still.

Mouse and Bunny stand at the edge, hand in hand. The dark is big tonight. Bunny holds on tight.

"One small step," says Mouse. "Soft and safe, soft and safe."

They take one small step.

Here is a firefly, a little green lamp, on and off, on and off. Here is another. Here is another after that. The dark is not empty. The dark is full of small lights.

Under the lilies, slow as a cloud, comes Mr. Trout. His silver side turns. His tail fans the quiet water.

"Good evening, small ones," says Mr. Trout. "The dark is only the day, lying down."

Here is the water: hush, hush, hush. Here is the cricket song, over and over. Here is a reed, leaning on its neighbour. Nothing here is sharp. Nothing here is loud.

Bunny lets go of the tight grip and sits down on the warm flat stone.

"Soft and safe, soft and safe," says Bunny.

Mouse sits too. They stay a while and name the things of the Pond. Here is the small boat of a leaf. Here is the ring a raindrop makes. Here is the glow of a firefly on the water, and the same glow again, upside down. Mr. Trout hums one low note under it all.

Then it is time to go home.

Up the path they go, slow and slower. The fireflies come partway, on and off, on and off.

Here is the door. Here is the warm room. Here is the blanket, turned back and waiting.

Two beds. Two blankets. Two sleepy heads down.

"Soft and safe," says Mouse. "Soft and safe," says Bunny.

Goodnight, path. Goodnight, Pond. Goodnight, firefly, with your little green lamp. Goodnight, reeds. Goodnight, crickets, singing on. Goodnight, Mr. Trout, deep and slow under the lilies. Goodnight, moon. Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

The dark is soft. The dark is kind. Sleep now.

This is the house voice. On a voice tier, the very same story is read in your voice, with your child's name woven in.
