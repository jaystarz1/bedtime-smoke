The Warmth We Sent Up

In Hush Hollow, when the sun goes down, the windows turn soft and yellow, and every small one is given something warm to hold.

That evening, Pup and Fox were given the big blanket. They took a corner each and went up the Hill, where the sky is biggest.

The grass was cool. The Hollow lay below them, low and glowing and quiet. They spread the blanket on the top of the Hill and sat down, shoulder to shoulder.

Warm and near, warm and near.

Then the first star came out.

"Hello, Wink," said Fox.

Wink brightened to hear it. But their light was thin tonight, and small, and a long way off.

"It is a wide sky up here," said Wink softly. "And I am the only one out yet."

Pup thought about that for a while. You cannot hand a blanket to a star. You cannot tuck a star into bed.

But there are other ways to send somebody warmth.

So Pup pulled the blanket over both their shoulders and said, "However far away you are, we will stay out here with you until the others come."

And Fox said, "And we will send you up some of ours."

So they did. They sat under the wool in the dark, and they hummed the low hum the Hollow hums at bedtime, and up it all went: the supper warmth, the blanket warmth, the warmth of two friends leaning in.

Warm and near, warm and near.

And Wink brightened. Not all at once. Slowly, the way any small light grows when somebody stays beside it.

"That reached me," said Wink. "All the way up, that reached me."

Then one by one the other stars came out around them, until there was a whole soft sky of small lights over the Hill, and nobody up there was alone. And the blanket was warmer for two than it had ever been for one.

"However far you go," said Wink, "I will keep the light on."

So Pup and Fox went back down the Hill with the blanket round them both, home to the Hollow, home to bed, warm and near, warm and near.

Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Wink, first light out, keeping the light on. Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

And however far you go, my love, the light stays on for you, and somebody is always sending you their warmth. Close your eyes now. The night will keep you.
