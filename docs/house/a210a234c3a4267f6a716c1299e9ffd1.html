Always Near, Always Dear

The Meadow is warm tonight. The tall grass is still.

Bunny and Hedgehog sit in the clover. Their den is a little way off, past the tall grass, where one small light waits.

"It is far," says Bunny.

"It is a little far," says Hedgehog.

Then Honey comes through the clover. Honey is the cat of Hush Hollow, and she finds the warm spot in every place. She winds once around Bunny. She winds once around Hedgehog. She settles down between them and begins to purr.

"You are not far," says Honey. "What you love, you carry. Always near, always dear."

"Always near, always dear," says Bunny.

So the three of them stay, and they name the Meadow, one small thing at a time.

Here is the clover, cool and round, holding the last of the day. Goodnight, clover.

Here is a firefly, coming up slow out of the grass. Goodnight, firefly.

Here is the tall grass, one seed head nodding, nodding, going still. Goodnight, tall grass.

Honey stretches long. She kneads the clover with her paws, the way cats do when a place is good. Her tail curls once around Hedgehog's ankle.

"Where is our den right now?" asks Hedgehog.

"In you," says Honey. "The warm blanket. The step at the door. Every goodnight anybody ever said to you. All of it is in there. Always near, always dear."

Hedgehog puts a hand flat over the heart. Warm. Bunny does the same. Warm there too.

"Always near, always dear," says Hedgehog, soft.

Here is a cricket, tuning one small string in the dark. Goodnight, cricket.

Here is a moth, going up and down over the grass tops, in no hurry at all. Goodnight, moth.

Here is a stone that held the sun all afternoon and kept a little of it. Two hands rest on it. Goodnight, warm stone.

Bunny yawns. Hedgehog yawns right after.

They lie back in the clover, side by side, with Honey folded warm between them, purring and purring. The Meadow leans over and holds still. Nobody is far from anything tonight. The den waits. The grass is a blanket. It is all the same warm.

Now the round of goodnights.

Goodnight, clover. Goodnight, firefly. Goodnight, tall grass. Goodnight, cricket. Goodnight, moth. Goodnight, warm stone. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Honey, purring in the warmest spot in the Hollow.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow. Sleep now. Always near, always dear.
