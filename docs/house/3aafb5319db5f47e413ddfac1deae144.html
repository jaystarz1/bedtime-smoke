Loved and Safe

Up on the Hill, the grass was soft and the evening came slow.

Fox spread the blanket wide. Pup sat down in the middle of it, small and still.

"The dark is very big," said Pup.

"It is," said Fox. "And we are in it together. Loved and safe, loved and safe."

Below them, Hush Hollow was going to bed. One window turned yellow. Then another. Then another, until the whole valley had little warm squares in it, like candles set out on a table.

"Who lights them?" asked Pup.

"Somebody who loves somebody," said Fox. "That is what a light in a window means."

Mr. Crow came down out of the darkening sky and settled on the fence post beside them. He folded his black wings, neat and slow, and rumbled low in his chest.

"Good evening, Mr. Crow," said Pup, politely.

"Good evening, small friend," said Mr. Crow. "The Hollow is quiet tonight. That is my favourite kind of night."

Pup leaned against Fox's arm. "What if the dark forgets us?"

"The dark is not a forgetting thing," said Fox. "It is a tucking-in thing. Here."

Fox held out one open hand. Over the grass, a firefly drifted up and blinked. Then two. Then a slow green scatter of them, on and off, on and off, all along the Hill.

"Oh," said Pup, softly.

"See how the dark keeps them?" said Fox. "It keeps us the same way. Loved and safe, loved and safe."

They stayed a while longer, with the fireflies going on and off and Mr. Crow rumbling to himself on the post. Then Fox folded the blanket into a square, and they went down the Hill path together, hand in hand, past the fence, past the gate, home.

The kitchen was warm. The stairs were quiet. Pup climbed under the quilt and the quilt came up snug, right to the chin.

Fox sat on the edge of the bed and hummed the low hum, the one with no words in it.

"Stay till I sleep?" said Pup.

"I am right here," said Fox. "Loved and safe, loved and safe."

Outside, the fireflies blinked slower. Mr. Crow tucked his head beneath his wing. The Hollow breathed out, and everything in it went soft.

Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Mr. Crow. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
