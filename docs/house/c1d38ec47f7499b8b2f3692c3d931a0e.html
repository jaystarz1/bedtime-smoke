Home, Home, Soft and Slow

In Hush Hollow, at the end of a long warm day, Pup and Fox went down to the Cove.

The water there is shallow and slow. It comes in, and it goes out, and it never hurries anyone.

They gathered smooth stones. They made a small ring of them. They sat side by side with their toes in the cool sand, and the sky above went from gold to grey to soft dark blue.

Then Pup gave a yawn. A wide one. The kind that means the day is done.

"Time to go up," said Fox. "Home, home, soft and slow."

But the path ran long, and Pup's legs were tired, and for one small moment Pup's chin wobbled.

Then a little light came bobbing over the water. Wink, with his lantern glowing warm and gold, his wings a tiny hum.

"I'll walk you," said Wink. "I always do. Wherever you go, however far, the Hollow keeps your bed warm and waiting."

So up they went. Wink first, glowing. Fox next. Pup last, one slow step at a time.

Home, home, soft and slow.

Past the reeds. Past the sleepy stones. Past the crickets tuning up in the grass.

And then, through the trees, a window. Warm and yellow and waiting, the way a window waits only for you.

"There," said Wink. "That light is yours."

Pup hurried the last little bit. Fox came right behind. And the door opened, and the warmth came out to meet them, and someone who loves them said the very best thing in the world:

"There you are."

Home, home, soft and slow.

Inside there was a blanket, and a cup of something warm, and a hand that smoothed Pup's hair back, once, twice, three times.

Outside, the Cove kept lapping. In and out. In and out.

And the Moon was up over Hush Hollow, quiet and kind, keeping her watch above the trees.

Wherever you go, my love, however far, home is waiting with the light on.

Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Wink, with your small gold lantern. Goodnight, Moon. Goodnight, Cove. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

Sleep now. Home, home, soft and slow.
