Look Low, Look Slow

The Garden went quiet and gold, the way it does when the day is nearly done. Pup and Fox sat on the warm stones by the bean rows, side by side, with the last of the light on their hands.

Then here came Honey, the Garden cat, winding slow between the flowers, and her purr had gone quiet. "I have lost my little bell," said Honey. "The small silver one. It was on my collar this morning, and now it is somewhere in the Garden, and the Garden is very big."

You know how it is when a small dear thing goes missing. The whole world goes a size too large.

"We will help you," said Pup. "We will help you," said Fox. "Nobody searches alone in Hush Hollow."

So the three of them went out into the Garden together, and they did not hurry. "Look low," said Honey. "Look slow." "Look low. Look slow," said Pup and Fox.

They went along the bean rows and parted the leaves gently, one and then the next. No bell. They went to the watering can and reached into its cool shadow. No bell. They went to Dandelion, out for her slow evening glide, and Dandelion tucked into her shell and was quiet a moment. "Try the soft dirt by the little gate," said Dandelion. "Small things roll downhill. Look low. Look slow."

Above them the Moon came up over the Garden wall, and the stars came out one at a time. Pup knelt by the little gate. Fox held the ferns back with both hands. And there in the soft dirt, half tucked under a curl of green, a small silver something caught the starlight.

"My bell," said Honey.

She pressed warm against them both, and her purr came back, and she gave them the slow blink that means I love you. "Thank you, you two," said Honey. "Nobody searches alone in Hush Hollow."

Then the three of them settled in the warm corner by the shed, where the straw is deep and the night air smells green and sweet. Pup gave a big, slow yawn. Fox gave a smaller one. Honey curled between them, warm as a fresh loaf, and the little bell gave one tiny sound and then hushed.

Look low. Look slow. And whatever is lost, we will find it together, and whoever is small will be held.

Now it is time for the goodnights. Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Honey, and the little silver bell. Goodnight, Dandelion. Goodnight, garden gate. Goodnight, moon. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
