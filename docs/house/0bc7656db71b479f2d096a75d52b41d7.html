Near and Dear

Down in the garden at the edge of Hush Hollow, the evening came in slow. The bean vines nodded. The path stones held the last of the warm day.

Hedgehog sat on the low garden wall, and Honey sat beside, big and warm and never in a hurry.

"Pup lives all the way over there," said Hedgehog, pointing past the beans, past the gate. "That's far. And Pup runs fast. I like sitting still."

"Mmm," Honey rumbled. "Shall we go and see how far?"

So they went down the path, soft and slow, soft and slow.

"Near and dear," Honey hummed as they went. "Near and dear."

At the beans, a small light blinked on. A firefly. Then two. Then a whole soft handful, drifting over the leaves.

"Pup can't see these," said Hedgehog. "Pup is too far away."

"Wait a moment," said Honey.

And there, on the other side of the beans, a small voice: "Fireflies!"

It was Pup, sitting still. Sitting still, just like Hedgehog. And the beans between them were only as wide as a hand.

"You're here," said Hedgehog.

"I'm here," said Pup. "The same lights come to my side too."

Honey settled down between them with a long warm sigh, and the two of them leaned in, one on each side.

"Near and dear," said Honey. "Near and dear."

The little lights went up and down over the beans.

"I ran three times round the gate today," said Pup.

"I watched one snail go all the way along the wall," said Hedgehog.

"Both good days," said Honey.

The crickets said the same thing over and over, which is a nice thing for crickets to say.

Then the Moon came up over the garden wall, and the garden went cool and quiet.

"Time to go in," said Honey. "I'm here. I'll walk you both."

So they walked back along the path, soft and slow, soft and slow, past the beans and the sleeping flowers, to the warm doorway of the Hollow.

Hedgehog yawned. Pup yawned too, at the same moment, which made them both smile.

Honey tucked a blanket over one, and a blanket over the other.

"Goodnight, Hedgehog," said Honey. "Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, beans and fireflies. Goodnight, Moon."

"Goodnight, Honey," said Hedgehog, small and sleepy. "Near and dear."

"Near and dear," said Honey. "Sleep now. I'm here."

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
