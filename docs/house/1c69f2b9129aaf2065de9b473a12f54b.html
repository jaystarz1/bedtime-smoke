The Last Warm Stone

The day let go of its light, and Sandy Cove went soft and grey. Small waves came in and went out again. Hush, hush. Hush, hush.

Bunny and Hedgehog sat together on the sand, down where the stream meets the calm shore.

All day the sun had lain along the stones. Now most of them had gone cool. But one stone, one smooth flat stone the colour of cream, still held the whole warm day inside it.

Bunny put a hand on it. Hedgehog put a hand on it too. The last warm one in the whole Cove. They meant to sit on it side by side while the water went dark.

Then, along the sand, came Honey.

She came the way she always comes: padding soft, tail up, winding once around Hedgehog's ankle and once around Bunny's. Honey knows every warm spot in Hush Hollow, and she had walked a long way looking for this one.

Bunny turned to Hedgehog. Hedgehog turned to Bunny. Neither of them said much. They only stood up, and stepped back, and left the last warm stone bare and waiting.

"For you," said Bunny. "For you, for you."

Honey stepped onto the stone. She kneaded it twice. She turned around once, and once more, and folded herself down into the middle of all that warmth, and began to purr.

The purr came up out of her, low and slow, and spread over the sand the way heat comes off a stone. Hush, hush, said the small waves. Hush, hush.

Bunny and Hedgehog settled in the cool sand beside her. The sand was cool. Their hands were cool. And still something warm sat quiet in the middle of them both, right where the giving had been.

Honey opened her eyes at them, slow, and shut them again, slow, the way she does.

"For you, for you," said Hedgehog, quietly, and put a hand on her back, and left it there.

The Moon came up over the water. The stream went on murmuring to the shore, and the shore went on hushing back. The little waves came slower. The sand held the shape of two small ones and one round curl of cat.

Bunny's eyes went heavy. Hedgehog's eyes went heavy. And the warm thing in the middle of them stayed, and stayed, and stayed, softer than sleep and just as easy.

Hush, hush, said the water. Hush, hush.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Honey, on the last warm stone.

Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
