The Slow Way to Night

The day in Hush Hollow had been a busy one.

Bunny had run the whole length of the meadow. Hedgehog had carried little pebbles, one by one, all the way to the garden wall. Now the sky went soft above the pond, and the water lay flat and shining.

But the busy was still going inside them.

"My feet are still running," said Bunny.

"My hands are still carrying," said Hedgehog.

At the edge of the pond, Mr. Trout rose in a ring of small ripples. His silver tail turned once, slow as a leaf on the water.

"Then let us put the day down," he said. "One piece at a time."

Slower now, softer now.

First Bunny put down the running. Bunny sat on the big flat stone and let both feet be still. The stone was warm from a whole day of sun.

Then Bunny slid over and made room, so Hedgehog could have the warmest part. Bunny did not say anything about it. The warm stone said it instead. It was a small, warm thing, and it stayed.

Hedgehog sat down and put the carrying down too. Hedgehog's hands rested, one on top of the other, and reached for nothing at all. The pond went slow. The sky went slow. Bunny and Hedgehog went slow with them.

Slower now, softer now.

Mr. Trout hushed the ripples, and the water went smooth. The reeds stopped their whispering and stood still. The little green frogs sang one more song, quieter, then no song at all. One firefly blinked, and blinked, and then held its small light steady. Nothing in the Hollow was in a hurry now.

Above the pond, the Moon came out over Hush Hollow, and the pond held her light like something kept safe. The day was put down now, piece by piece, and nothing was waiting to be picked back up.

Slower now, softer now.

Bunny's eyes were heavy. Hedgehog's head tipped gently sideways. The warm stone held them both, side by side, in the last of the day's warmth.

"Goodnight, Bunny," said Mr. Trout, soft as water. "Goodnight, Hedgehog."

And down he went, slow and easy, into the quiet dark of the pond.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Mr. Trout. Goodnight, Moon, over the still, still water. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

Sleep now. Soft and slow. Goodnight.
