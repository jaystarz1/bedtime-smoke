Goodnight, Dandelion

The Garden in Hush Hollow is warm tonight. The Garden is still.

Mouse sits on the flat gray stone. Bunny sits beside Mouse. They are waiting for the dandelion to open her seeds.

"Not yet," says Dandelion. Her stem sways. Her small green leaves lift and settle. "Soon. Slow and soon."

"Slow and soon," says Mouse.

"Slow and soon," says Bunny.

So they wait.

Here is a snail on a wet leaf, going nowhere in particular. Goodnight, small snail.

Here is a bean vine holding the fence with both hands. Goodnight, bean vine.

Here is a watering can, tipped and empty and cool. Goodnight, watering can.

Dandelion sways. She is not ready. That is all right.

"Slow and soon," says Dandelion.

"Slow and soon," say Mouse and Bunny together.

So they wait some more.

Here is a row of round radishes, sleeping under the dirt. Goodnight, radishes.

Here is a beetle in the mint, holding still. Goodnight, beetle.

Here is one warm crack in the stone where the day is stored up. Mouse puts a hand there. Bunny puts a hand there too.

The air goes dim. The crickets start their small song. Mouse yawns. Bunny yawns after.

And then Dandelion opens.

It is quiet. It is soft. Her seeds come out one at a time, white and round, each one on a thin little stem, like a tiny lamp turning on.

Mouse says nothing. Bunny says nothing. They only sit, side by side, while Dandelion turns from yellow into a soft white crown of seed.

"There," says Dandelion. "You waited so well."

"Slow and soon," says Mouse, sleepy now.

"Slow and soon," says Bunny, sleepier still.

One seed lifts. It goes up over the fence and out into the dark, off to be a new dandelion somewhere. The rest stay put till morning.

Mouse and Bunny go home along the garden path, soft and slow. Their beds are warm. Their blankets are already turned back.

Now the round of goodnights.

Goodnight, Dandelion, standing white in the dark. Goodnight, snail. Goodnight, bean vine. Goodnight, radishes under the dirt. Goodnight, watering can. Goodnight, {{moon}}.

Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Bunny.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow. Sleep now. Slow and soon.
