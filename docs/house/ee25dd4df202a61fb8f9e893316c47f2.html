One Small Step

In Hush Hollow the garden went gold, and then went grey, and then went soft and dark.

Mouse and Bunny stood at the end of the bean row, hand in hand. Tonight the moonflower would open, and neither of them had ever stayed out in the garden that late before. The path ahead was quiet, and dark, and new.

Then Honey came padding down the row with her tail up, purring her low warm purr. She leaned warm against their ankles and gave them the slow blink that means I love you.

"One small step, soft and slow," said Honey.

So Mouse took one small step. So Bunny took one small step. The dirt was warm under them, and the garden did not mind at all.

They took another step, and a beetle tucked in under a leaf beside them.

And the beetle is snug, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

They took another step, and a cricket folded his song down to a hum.

And the cricket is snug, and the beetle is snug, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

They took another step, and a moth came down and rested her wings.

And the moth is snug, and the cricket is snug, and the beetle is snug, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

"One small step, soft and slow," said Honey, walking beside them.

They took another step, and the sparrow on the fence made a soft feather pile of herself.

And the sparrow is snug, and the moth is snug, and the cricket is snug, and the beetle is snug, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

They took one more step, and there at the end of the row stood the moonflower, holding itself shut tight.

Mouse waited. Bunny waited. And the moonflower opened, white and wide, the way a small hand opens.

They had done it, one small step at a time.

The Moon came up over Hush Hollow, quiet and kind.

Mouse sat down. Bunny sat down. Honey stretched long and settled between them, purring, warm as a pocket.

"One small step, soft and slow," said Mouse and Bunny and Honey, and then nobody said anything more.

And the beetle is asleep, and the cricket is asleep, and the moth is asleep, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

And the sparrow is asleep, and the bean leaves are asleep, and the moonflower is wide and white and still, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

And Honey is asleep, purring low, and Mouse is asleep, and Bunny is asleep, warm hand to warm hand, all snug in the dark, kind garden.

Goodnight, moonflower. Goodnight, beetle and cricket and moth. Goodnight, sparrow on the fence. Goodnight, bean leaves. Goodnight, Honey. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Mouse. Goodnight, Bunny.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
