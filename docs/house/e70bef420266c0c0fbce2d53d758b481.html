Goodnight, Hush Hollow

Night came soft to the Wood, the way it always does.

Pup and Fox sat side by side on the wide flat stone near the old stump, where Bear kept his lantern. Bear was a big warm shadow behind them, humming low, and his rumble went through the stone and into their backs like a slow drum.

But Fox's thoughts were going fast tonight. Round and round they went, quick as leaves in a little wind. Did I put the blue cup back? Is tomorrow a rainy day? What if I cannot sleep?

"My thinking won't stop," Fox said.

Pup reached over and held Fox's hand.

"Then we will breathe," said Pup. "In and out, soft and slow."

So they did. Pup counted, one, two, three, and the air came in. One, two, three, and the air went out.

In and out, soft and slow.

Bear's lantern glowed honey-gold. A cricket kept a small tidy beat in the grass. Above the branches, the Moon kept her own quiet watch, the way she keeps it every single night.

"Again," said Pup.

One, two, three, in. One, two, three, out. Fox's shoulders came down. The fast thoughts came slower, the way a swing slows when nobody pushes it anymore.

In and out, soft and slow.

"Where do the worries go?" Fox asked.

"They float," said Bear, in his deep rumbly voice. "Up past the leaves. The night is very big. It has room for all of them, and it has room for you, too, and it will hold you the whole way till morning."

Fox breathed one more time, and the last worry went up and up and was gone.

Then came the walk home, which was a short one. Bear carried the lantern. Pup and Fox went beside him through the grass, slower and slower, until the walking was mostly leaning.

At the door of the warm burrow-house, Bear set the lantern down low.

"One more," whispered Pup.

In and out, soft and slow.

Fox yawned. Pup yawned too, because yawns are catching. They lay down under the soft blanket, side by side, warm all the way through, while the little light went dim, and dimmer, and dimmer still.

Whatever the day was, however fast your thinking went, the night comes and holds you. It always does. It always will.

Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Bear, and goodnight, lantern. Goodnight, cricket in the grass. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.

In and out.

Soft.

And slow.
