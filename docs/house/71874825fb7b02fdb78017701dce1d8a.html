The Day Lies Down

In Hush Hollow, the stream runs down to the Sandy Cove. There the water is calm, and the small waves come in, hush, hush, and slip away again.

Pup and Fox had played at the cove all day long. They gathered pink shells. They jumped the small waves, again and again.

Now the sky was turning soft and gold. But Pup and Fox were still buzzing.

"One more wave!" said Pup. "One more shell!" said Fox. "I can't stop," said Pup. "I'm all fizzy inside."

Out on the water, a quiet ring of ripples spread, slow and wide. Up rose Mr. Trout, who keeps the water calm and kind.

"Good evening," he burbled. "What a fine, full day. Shall we help it lie down?" "How?" asked Fox. "One thing at a time," said Mr. Trout. "I'm right here. Little by little, the day lies down."

So Pup and Fox sat down on the cool sand, side by side.

"First, the waves," said Mr. Trout. "Whisper them goodnight." "Goodnight, waves," whispered Pup and Fox. Hush, came the waves, a bit smaller. Hush, came the waves, smaller still.

"Now, the shells." Pup and Fox laid them on a flat, warm stone, one by one, to sleep.

"Now, your busy feet and hands." The jumping feet grew still. The reaching hands grew still. Toes wiggled in the sand, just once, and rested too.

The birds on the rocks tucked in and went quiet.

"It's getting dark," said Fox softly. "The dark is only the night, come to tuck in the day," said Mr. Trout. "It is soft and kind here. I'm right here. Little by little, the day lies down."

One star came out. Then another. Then too many to count, all shining in the still water.

The cove was quiet now. The waves said hush, and nothing more. Calm came down over the sand like a blanket, and all the fizz inside Pup and Fox settled, slow and soft.

"Time to rest," said Mr. Trout. "I'll stay right here in the shallows until you sleep."

So Pup and Fox lay down, hand in hand, in a snug hollow of dry sand, up from the water. The sand there was warm from the whole long day.

Pup gave a big, slow yawn. Fox gave a smaller one.

Mr. Trout glided along the edge of the water, sending up quiet rings of ripples. I'm right here, said the ripples. I'm right here.

"Little by little, the day lies down," murmured Mr. Trout. And Pup and Fox closed their eyes. Then Mr. Trout sank into the cool, calm water to rest.

Goodnight, Pup. Goodnight, Fox. Goodnight, Mr. Trout, in the cool, calm water. Goodnight, small waves. Goodnight, sleepy shells. Goodnight, stars. Goodnight, Moon. Little by little, the day lies down. Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
