Right Beside You

Evening came to Hush Hollow the way it always does, soft and unhurried, with the lamps in the windows turning gold.

Bunny and Hedgehog went up the Hill together while there was still a little light left. The grass on the Hill is long and cool, and the finest thing in the whole world is to lie down at the top and roll all the way to the bottom, laughing the whole way down.

The big ones were already there, rolling fast and laughing. At the edge of the long grass sat Little Wren, small as a thimble, with her feathers all fluffed. Every time she started up the Hill, the rolling ones came whirling past, and she stepped back into the grass to wait.

Bunny stopped. Bunny walked over through the long grass and sat down beside Little Wren.

"Right beside you," Bunny said. "Soft and slow."

Hedgehog came too, and sat on the other side, so Little Wren had one on each side of her, warm as new bread.

Then Hedgehog stood up and lifted a hand to the big ones at the top of the Hill. "There's a small one rolling down," Hedgehog said. "We're all going soft and slow."

Mr. Crow, up on the fencepost, ruffled his black wings and gave one long croak, the sort that carries. The rolling ones slowed. They waited. They made a wide grassy lane straight down the middle of the Hill, just for her.

Little Wren went down that lane like a leaf on water, tumbling and tumbling, with Bunny on one side and Hedgehog on the other, all three of them going soft and slow.

"Right beside you," Bunny said at the bottom. "Soft and slow," Hedgehog said. And Little Wren laughed a small laugh, about the size of a bell.

They went down again. And again. And again, until the grass turned grey and the lamps were the brightest thing on the Hill.

Then home along the path, into the Hollow. Doors clicked shut. Blankets came up. Somewhere in the dark, Little Wren was tucked in her own small nest, not alone at all, and the whole Hollow was a little warmer for it.

Bunny yawned a long yawn. Hedgehog yawned a longer one. The night went soft and slow.

Goodnight, Bunny. Goodnight, Hedgehog. Goodnight, Mr. Crow, up on your fencepost. Goodnight, Little Wren, in your small warm nest. Goodnight, Moon.

Goodnight, Hush Hollow.
